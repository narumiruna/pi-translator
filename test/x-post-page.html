Today we announce 1Password for Agents platform, building with companies @Muse, @AnthropicAI, and @OpenAI, with more integrations on the way. We've heard from customers who want personal AI agents to help them get more done, but want more control over what the agent can access. 1Password for Agents gives your agents access without giving up your passwords. When an agent reaches a sign-in form, it requests a credential from your vault. If you approve the request, 1Password directly auto-fills the credential in the destination site. The agent gets the task done, but it does not get your passwords. Unless you opt in to an agent connector, agents cannot touch your vault.
