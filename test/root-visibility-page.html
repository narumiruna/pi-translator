A readable cached manifesto

The cached article remains readable when the remote document loads too late.

  1. Readers must be able to understand the original text and its translation.
  2. A hidden duplicate must not prevent translation of the visible article.

A readable embedded manifesto

The embedded document has more semantic blocks than the cached article.

  1. Readers must be able to understand the original text and its translation.
  2. A hidden duplicate must not prevent translation of the visible article.
  3. The remote document can arrive after the local fallback has been shown.
  4. The embedded article can also load successfully before the timeout.
  5. Hidden content must not receive source identifiers or translation notes.
  6. Visible offscreen articles must remain eligible for root selection.
  7. Repeated translation must not include text from existing translation notes.
  8. Both desktop and narrow mobile layouts must remain readable.
